# Comparison Selection Reference

Pick the comparison closest to the actual value. Round to friendly numbers.

## Energy (Wh)

| Range | Use |
|-------|-----|
| < 0.5 Wh | "A few seconds of an LED bulb" |
| 0.5–2 Wh | "[X] minutes of an LED bulb" |
| 2–10 Wh | "[X] minutes of an LED bulb" or "~1/10–1/2 of a phone charge" |
| 10–25 Wh | "About [fraction] of a phone charge" or "[X] min of laptop use" |
| 25–60 Wh | "1–3 phone charges" or "~[X] min of laptop use" |
| 60–100 Wh | "[X] phone charges" or "about an hour of laptop use" |
| 100+ Wh | "[X] hours of a 55\" TV" or "[X] phone charges" |

Reference: LED bulb = 10W · Phone charge = 20 Wh (modern smartphone avg; small phones ~15 Wh, large Androids ~25 Wh) · Laptop = 50 Wh/hr · 55" LED TV = 100 Wh/hr

## Water (mL)

| Range | Use |
|-------|-----|
| < 2 mL | "A few drops" |
| 2–8 mL | "About [fraction] of a teaspoon" |
| 8–20 mL | "[X] teaspoons" or "about a tablespoon" |
| 20–60 mL | "[X] tablespoons" or "about [fraction] of a cup" |
| 60–150 mL | "About [fraction] of a cup" or "[X] shot glasses" |
| 150–400 mL | "About [fraction] of a water bottle" |
| 400+ mL | "Nearly/more than a water bottle" |

Reference: Teaspoon = 5 mL · Tablespoon = 15 mL · Shot = 45 mL · Cup = 250 mL · Bottle = 500 mL
